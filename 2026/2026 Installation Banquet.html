2026 Property-Type Dynamics: Single-Family vs. Condominiums

Why total closings jumped +13.3% while the combined median price held at $525,000:

Single-Family Homes

Continued price appreciation (+2.4% YoY to $430,000 region-wide), rising pending sales (+5.3%), and tightening active inventory (-12.7%).

Condos & Townhomes
