Highlights from this issue

A quick look at the features, market stats, ballot guides, and history shaping our Fall 2026 edition.

Leadership & Vision

Jeanne’s Journal: From Promise to Progress

CCOR President Jeanne Drath reflects on turning strategic conversations into concrete action, elevating professionalism, our Protect Our Homes partnership with the Lee County Clerk of Courts, and championing the Bonita Estero Rail Trail (BERT).

See pages 3–4
Events & Community

Coconut Coast Kickoff & New Board Leadership

A vibrant recap of the August 13 CCOR Kickoff with 84 attendees celebrating our new identity, ribbon cutting, and live music, plus welcoming Andy Brinkoetter and Jolyn Covelli to Board leadership.

See pages 4–6
Advocacy in Action

Florida Votes 2026: Three Ballot Amendments

A plain-language guide to the November 3 general election constitutional amendments requiring 60% approval: Amendment 1 (Budget Stabilization Fund), Amendment 2 (Agricultural Tangible Property), and Amendment 3 (Property Taxes).

See page 8
Market Snapshot

Q2 2026 Market Stats: Bonita Springs & Estero

Second quarter indicators reveal 1,086 closed sales outperforming both 2025 and 2024, median prices holding steady in the mid-$500Ks, 63 median days on market, and 337 pending sales leading in ZIP 34135.

See page 9
Property Tax Deep Dive

Property Tax Reset? Understanding Amendment 3

A detailed look at proposed Amendment 3: phased non-school homestead exemptions, special rules for new residents arriving after 2026, lowering the non-homestead assessment cap from 10% to 5%, and municipal revenue effects.

See page 10
Transaction Guidance

What Amendment 3 Means in a Transaction

Essential compliance practices for REALTORS®: why a seller’s tax bill is never the buyer’s future bill, avoiding tax predictions, safe phrasing, and utilizing the official referral directory for property appraisers and CPAs.

See page 11
Anniversary Series

Striving for 60: Chapter 3 — A Region Transformed

Covering 1996–2002: FGCU’s opening in 1997, Bonita Springs incorporating on Dec 31, 1999, the creation of SunshineMLS with Naples in 2002, 1999 Board President John Mitchell, and the emergence of an interconnected regional market.

See page 12
Development & Resilience

Growth Accelerates, Then the Market Turns (2003–2010)

The opening of RSW’s 28-gate terminal (2005) and Coconut Point (2006), followed by Hurricanes Charley and Wilma, the 2007–2010 housing correction, and how agents adapted through SFR certification and digital tools.

See page 13
From the Archives

Tools of the Trade: 1996–2010

A retrospective on the technology transformation: desktop PCs with dial-up modems, CD-ROM databases, early online MLS, 128MB digital camera cards, early virtual tours, PalmPilots, Thomas Guides, and electronic lockboxes.

See page 14
Disaster Preparedness

Storm-Ready Transactions: Before the Cone

Pre-storm readiness protocols: assembling roof permits, opening protection photos, and elevation certs before listing; binding insurance early, verifying wires, and understanding the 30-day NFIP waiting period.

See page 15
State Policy & Funding

What Changed This Summer? Florida Housing Update

Key appropriations from Florida’s 2026–27 budget: $378M for My Safe Florida Home, $27M for My Safe Florida Condo, $236.5M for SHIP & SAIL, $50M for Hometown Heroes, and HB 1389 Live Local amendments.

See page 17
Condo Compliance

The Hidden Closing Hurdle: Condo Underwriting

WWMR attorney Brian A. Cordero, Esq. details how updated 2026 Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines scrutinize reserve studies, structural milestone inspections, and deferred maintenance across Florida’s 900,000+ older units.

From the Coconut Coast to Rio: CILA 2026

President Jeanne Drath represented CCOR at CILA 2026 and CIMI360 in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, building international relationships and referral pipelines with one of Florida's premier foreign buyer markets.
