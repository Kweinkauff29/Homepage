2026 Qualification Criteria & Working Thresholds

Preliminary sales excellence eligibility is measured across MLS production for the award year. Current preliminary benchmark requirements:

Total Sales Volume: More than $5,000,000
Total Transaction Sides: More than 25 sides
Standard: Both thresholds required (AND logic)
Auditing & Verification in Progress: Preliminary qualification records are currently undergoing verification by CCOR staff. Official award recipients and tier designations will be published upon conclusion of the qualification and audit period.
